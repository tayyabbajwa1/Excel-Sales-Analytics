# 📊 AtliQ Hardwares — Sales & Finance Analytics | Excel

An end-to-end **Microsoft Excel analytics project** developed for **AtliQ Hardwares**, a fictional consumer electronics company. The project focuses on transforming raw business data into actionable insights through **data cleaning, modeling, analysis, and interactive reporting**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project consists of two major analytical reports:

* 💰 **Sales Analytics Report**
* 💼 **Finance Analytics Report**

The reports are designed to help business stakeholders monitor performance, identify trends, evaluate targets, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 💰 Sales Analytics Report

### 🎯 Business Objectives

The Sales Report was developed to:

1. Create a [**Customer Performance Report**](./Customer-Performance-Report.pdf) to evaluate sales across customers.
2. Compare [**Market Performance vs. Sales Targets**](./Market-Performance-vs-Target-Report.pdf) to identify performance gaps.
3. Develop a [**Top 10 Products Report**](./Top-10-Products-Report.pdf) to identify the highest-performing products.
4. Create a [**Division-Level Sales Report**](./Division-Level-Report.pdf) to analyze performance across business divisions.
5. Compare [**Quantity Sold by Top 5 vs. Bottom 5 Products**](./Quantity-Sold-by-Top-and-Bottom-5-Products.pdf) to identify product performance differences.

### 📈 Business Value

**Purpose of Sales Analytics**

Enable management to monitor sales performance, identify growth opportunities, and make informed commercial decisions.

**Importance of Sales Analysis**

* Identify sales trends and patterns.
* Track important **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**.
* Evaluate customer and product performance.
* Identify markets with growth potential.
* Monitor performance against sales targets.

**Role of the Reports**

The insights generated can support:

* Customer discount negotiations.
* Strategic pricing decisions.
* Customer performance evaluation.
* Identification of high-potential markets.
* Business expansion and growth strategies.

---

# 💼 Finance Analytics Report

### 🎯 Business Objectives

The Finance Report was developed to:

1. Create **Profit & Loss (P&L) Reports** by Fiscal Year and Month.
2. Analyze **P&L Performance by Market**.
3. Analyze **Gross Margin % (GM%) by Quarter and Sub-Zone**.

### 📈 Business Value

**Purpose of Finance Analytics**

Provide visibility into financial performance and support strategic decision-making by management and stakeholders.

**Importance of Financial Analysis**

* Evaluate profitability and financial performance.
* Compare performance across markets and time periods.
* Support budgeting and forecasting activities.
* Identify changes in gross margin and profitability.
* Enable performance benchmarking.

**Role of the Reports**

The financial insights help stakeholders:

* Align financial planning with business objectives.
* Identify profitable and underperforming markets.
* Monitor profitability trends.
* Support strategic financial decisions.
* Improve confidence in financial planning and forecasting.
