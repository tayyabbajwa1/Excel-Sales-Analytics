# 📊 AtliQ Hardwares — Sales & Finance Analytics | Excel

An end-to-end **Microsoft Excel analytics project** developed for **AtliQ Hardwares**, a fictional consumer electronics company. The project demonstrates how raw business data can be transformed into meaningful insights through **ETL, data modeling, DAX, and business-focused reporting**.

The project focuses on two key business functions:

- 💰 **Sales Analytics**
- 💼 **Finance Analytics**

The objective is to provide stakeholders with actionable insights that support performance evaluation, strategic decision-making, and business growth.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project was designed to address key business questions related to:

- Customer and product performance
- Market performance against sales targets
- Sales quantity trends
- Division-level performance
- Profitability and financial performance
- Gross margin trends across markets and sub-zones

The analysis combines **Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, and Excel-based data modeling** to create structured and user-focused analytical reports.

---

# 💰 Sales Analytics

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The Sales Analytics report was developed to:

1. Create a [Customer Performance Report](./Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf) to evaluate sales performance across customers.

2. Compare [Market Performance vs. Sales Targets](./Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf) to identify performance gaps and target achievement.

3. Develop a [Top 10 Products Report](./Top%2010%20Products%20Report.pdf) to identify the highest-performing products.

4. Create a [Division-Level Sales Report](./Division%20Level%20Report.pdf) to analyze sales performance across business divisions.

5. Compare [Quantity Sold by Top and Bottom 5 Products](./Quantity%20Sold%20by%20Top%20and%20Bottom%205%20Products.pdf) to understand differences between the best- and worst-performing products.

## 📈 Business Value

### Purpose of Sales Analytics

Sales analytics enables management to monitor sales activities, evaluate performance, identify trends, and make informed commercial decisions.

### Importance of Sales Analysis

The analysis helps to:

- Identify sales trends and patterns.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Evaluate customer and product performance.
- Measure actual sales against targets.
- Identify high-potential markets and products.
- Support strategic sales planning.

### Role of Sales Reports

The reports can support business decisions related to:

- Customer discount strategies.
- Pricing and commercial negotiations.
- Customer performance evaluation.
- Product portfolio optimization.
- Identification of promising markets.
- Business expansion opportunities.

# 💼 Finance Analytics

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The Finance Analytics report was developed to:

1. Analyze [P & L Performance by Fiscal Year](./P%20%26%20L%20Statement%20by%20Fiscal%20Year.pdf) to evaluate annual financial performance.

2. Analyze [P & L Performance by Month](./P%20%26%20L%20Statement%20by%20Months.pdf) to identify monthly profitability trends.

3. Analyze [P & L Performance by Market](./P%20%26%20L%20Statement%20by%20Markets.pdf) to evaluate profitability across different markets.

4. Analyze [Gross Margin % by Quarter and Sub-Zone](./GM%20%25%20by%20Quarters%20for%20Sub-Zone.pdf) to monitor profitability trends across geographical sub-zones.

## 📈 Business Value

### Purpose of Finance Analytics

Finance analytics provides visibility into profitability and financial performance while supporting strategic planning and data-driven decision-making.

### Importance of Financial Analysis

The analysis helps to:

- Evaluate financial performance over time.
- Identify profitable and underperforming markets.
- Monitor gross margin trends.
- Compare performance across fiscal periods.
- Support budgeting and forecasting.
- Enable performance benchmarking.

### Role of Finance Reports

The reports help stakeholders:

- Align financial planning with strategic objectives.
- Identify areas of profitability improvement.
- Monitor financial performance.
- Support strategic business decisions.
- Improve financial planning and forecasting.
